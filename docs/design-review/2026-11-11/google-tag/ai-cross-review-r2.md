[1/3] In en/privacy.html, the Consent Mode v2 snippet grants ad_storage, ad_user_data, and ad_personalization (and leaves them unblocked outside EEA/UK/CH) despite the privacy policy stating no advertising cookies are used; the privacy policy also lacks a mechanism to withdraw consent (and gives misleading advice about clearing cookies when consent is replayed from localStorage) and retains a contradictory leftover sentence claiming nothing is loaded or set unless live chat is opened.
[2/3] `js/consent-banner.js` has two functional bugs: the timezone regex places the trailing slash outside the outer group so none of the `Atlantic/*` EEA timezones (Iceland, Canary Islands, Madeira, Azores, Faroe) ever match (and Cyprus `Asia/Nicosia`/`Asia/Famagusta` is omitted despite `CY` being in the Consent Mode `region` list), and `document.querySelector('a[href*="privacy"]')` on `privacy.html` matches the header/menu language switcher (`href="/en/privacy"`) before the actual privacy policy link (`href="/privacy"`), sending users to the wrong language page.
[3/3] The Round 2 changes in tools/copy.mjs, tools/google-tag.mjs, tools/make-dist.mjs, and tools/verify-site.mjs properly inject Consent Mode v2 defaults before the GTM snippet, enforce single-tag presence at build and verification time, and update the bilingual privacy policy disclosures. Only minor follow-up improvements were found.
