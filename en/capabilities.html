01 · DISCOVER
Find market opportunities. AI keeps looking through public markets, search, social platforms, trade data, dealer networks and company signals.
Not waiting for inquiries — looking for demand as it happens.
02 · QUALIFY
Decide who is worth following. Growth OS scores company background, buying signals, target market, product fit, decision chain and past behaviour.
Sales time goes to the people who can actually close.
03 · ENGAGE
Reach out. Personalised content by company, market, role, buying scenario and product need; Email, WhatsApp, Alibaba and the website share one context.
Many channels, one context.
04 · UNDERSTAND
Every conversation becomes customer intelligence: who, which market, what they want, intent, expected demand, key questions, history, risk signals and next step.
Every exchange updates Customer 360.
05 · RESPOND
Reply only after AI understands your product — from the knowledge base, product database, past quotes, business rules and customer context.
Simple questions handled, complex ones approved by a human, unknowns never invented.
06 · QUOTE
From inquiry to quote without opening Excel. Quote Studio drafts from customer, product, configuration, quantity, pricing rules, past deals, margin guardrails and trade terms.
Price-sensitive items go to approval, then formal quote and PI.
07 · EXECUTE
From quote to a real order: PI, order, payment milestones, production, QC, packing, shipment, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, Form E, bill of lading, certifications, export and tax-rebate flow.
Sales and fulfilment on one chain for the first time.
08 · FOLLOW
AI doesn’t forget a customer. No reply, no decision after the quote, a conversation from six months ago, a stack of trade-show cards — Dormant Lead Reactivation, Trade Show Afterburner and Follow-up Agents keep looking for the moment to reopen.
Every lead gets a next time.
09 · LEARN
Every outcome makes the next run better. Which customers closed, which market converts, which outreach works, which quotes were accepted — outcomes return to the Evolution Engine.
The system doesn’t just store data; it learns how the company wins business.

Find the buyers worth pursuing

Start with your products and target markets. Company research, available trade records and buying signals come together into an account you can judge, not a contact list.

  • Account Research: Collects company evidence and cites where it came from
  • Trade Intelligence: Reads available trade records for demand and direction
  • Importer Reorder Radar: Estimates which importers may be due to reorder
  • Buying Committee Intelligence: Who decides, who influences and who signs
  • Opportunity Decision Engine: Recommends pursue, park or drop, with the reason
  • Dealer Opportunity Brief: A one-page case for approaching a distributor
  • CRM Automatic Lead Creation: Writes the qualified account into CRM with an owner

Useful output: An evidence-backed account brief, an outreach draft and a follow-up task with an owner.

Connects to: The same account context carries into the next conversation and quotation.

Turn conversations into customer understanding

Inquiries arrive from connected channels and land on one customer timeline. Requirements are read out of the message, answered from approved company knowledge, and anything sensitive waits for a person.

  • Unified Inbox: Every channel lands in one queue with the customer attached
  • Inquiry Intent Detection: Separates a real buying request from noise
  • Buyer Requirement Extraction: Pulls product, spec, quantity and terms out of free text
  • Customer Risk Signals: Flags payment, compliance and credibility concerns early
  • Knowledge-Grounded Reply: Drafts the answer from approved company sources
  • Account 360: Everything known about the account on one screen
  • Automatic Follow-up: Sends the next planned touch when nothing comes back

Useful output: A customer record, structured requirements and a reply ready for review.

Connects to: The agreed requirement becomes the starting point of the quotation.

Prepare quotations with commercial control

A quotation starts from the inquiry, the approved product information and your configured pricing rules — not a blank spreadsheet. AI prepares; authorized people commit.

  • Quote Studio: Builds the quotation from the inquiry, not a blank sheet
  • Product Configuration & Quantity: What exactly is being priced, and how many
  • Pricing Rules: Prices from your configured rules, not from guesswork
  • Margin Guardrails: Stops a quote crossing the margin line without approval
  • Historical Price Context: Shows what this buyer and market paid before
  • Approval Workflow: Routes the exception to the person allowed to decide
  • PI Studio / PI Center: Turns the approved quote into a pro forma invoice

Useful output: An approved quotation, its version history and the pro forma invoice behind it.

Connects to: Approved commercial detail passes into the order without re-entry.

Coordinate orders through fulfilment and follow-up

The order carries payment milestones, production status, packing and shipment in one place, and the commercial paperwork is prepared from approved order data.

  • Order Management: Tracks the order from approved quote to delivery
  • Payment Milestones: Tracks deposits, balances and what is still outstanding
  • Production Status & QC: Where the goods are, and whether they passed
  • Commercial Invoice · Packing List: Prepared from approved order data, ready for review
  • Certificate of Origin · Form E: Organizes the application material; issuance stays with the authority
  • Bill of Lading Workflow: Keeps shipping documents moving with the shipment
  • Export Documentation & Workflow: Export documentation and workflow

Useful output: A visible order status, a document checklist and the next accountable owner.

Connects to: Delivery outcomes stay on the account for after-sales and the next purchase.

Document preparation and workflow support do not replace official issuance, customs decisions or professional compliance review.

What every story runs on

Four things every story above depends on.

Business context

Customers, quotes and orders are objects with relationships and rules, and answers are retrieved from company sources rather than improvised.

  • Operational Ontology: Customers, quotes and orders as objects AI can act on
  • Customer · Product · Inquiry · Opportunity Objects: The commercial side of the business, as data
  • Enterprise Brain: The approved company answer, in one place
  • Enterprise RAG: Retrieves the passage an answer is based on
  • Source-Grounded Answers: No answer without a source your team approved

Tools and connections

Agents reach existing systems through documented interfaces, and work the tools that have no interface at all.

  • API Actions · MCP Client · MCP Tools: Documented interfaces an agent is allowed to call
  • External Connectors · Workspace Bridge: Reaches the systems your team already runs
  • Activepieces Workflow Automation: Connects steps across apps without custom code
  • Browser Automation · Playwright: Works the web tools that have no API
  • Credential Management: Holds the logins so they never enter a prompt

A connector in the register is not a live integration for your company until it is configured and authorized.

Authority and evidence

The company sets what may run alone, what needs review and what needs approval; every important action leaves a record a person can read back.

  • Human-in-the-Loop: Names the decisions a person must still make
  • Approval Service: One place where pending decisions wait for their owner
  • Permission Control · Identity: Permission control and identity
  • Audit Ledger · Agent Evidence · Action History: What was done, by which agent, on whose authority
  • Failure Handling · Rollback: Failure handling and rollback

In the catalogue: 13 Governance & Control

Improvement you can check

Recorded outcomes become candidate changes to knowledge, prompts, skills and workflows — evaluated and approved before release, rolled back when they do not hold.

  • Observer Agent: Watches real execution and records what happened
  • Trajectory · Outcome · Evaluation: What was attempted, what resulted, how it scored
  • Reflection Scientist: Turns recorded outcomes into candidate improvements
  • Eval & Red Team: Tests a change, and tries to break it, before release
  • Champion / Challenger · Canary · Rollback: Test a change on a slice, keep it or take it back

This improves knowledge, prompts, skills and workflows. It does not retrain foundation-model weights on its own.
