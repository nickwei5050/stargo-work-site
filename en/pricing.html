(Questions and answers)

About pricing

Are the 288 AI employees unlimited?
No. The workforce count describes the capability catalog. Actual access, active workloads, concurrency, credits and third-party usage are set by your contract.
What happens after the first year?
The software subscription renews annually. Domain, hosting and ongoing production are billed separately, under the renewal proposal or at actual third-party cost. A first-year launch package is not a promise of repeated annual content production.
What does Standard include?
A 12-month cloud workspace (up to 5 standard users), an initial import of enterprise knowledge and product information (up to 20 SKUs), inquiries and CRM, quotes with human approval, self-service lead discovery with CRM entry, standard annual AI credits, plus one setup session and one training session.
Is customer acquisition only in the ¥40,000 package?
No. Standard already includes self-service acquisition: lead discovery, company profiling, scoring, outreach preparation and CRM entry. Global Acquisition adds customer acquisition set up and run for three months, plus three monthly reports and its own website and content deliverables.
Is private deployment available?
Enterprise offers a dedicated environment and private deployment for companies with stricter data, system and compliance requirements.
Can it connect to our CRM or ERP?
Yes. Email, drives, CRM, ERP and business platforms connect with your authorization; what can be read, what can be changed and what needs approval is agreed with you item by item. Migration is part of Enterprise.
Are model costs included?
Platform fees and model / API / third-party usage are billed separately; each plan includes its own credits, with overage billed on use.
How do training and implementation work?
Standard includes one setup session and one basic training session. Enterprise comes with a dedicated FDE who feeds real workflows straight back into the platform.
Which level should we start at?
Start with one workflow. Pick the work that costs the most time or growth, get it running, then decide which level you need.
What about multiple companies or brands?
Multiple departments, companies, brands and accounts belong to Enterprise: separate permissions, approvals and data boundaries on one shared AI workforce.
