(Questions and answers)

About pricing

Are the 288 AI employees unlimited?
No. The workforce count describes the capability catalogue. Actual access, active workloads, concurrency, credits and third-party usage depend on the contracted configuration.
What happens after the first year?
The software subscription follows its annual renewal terms. Domain, hosting and ongoing production follow the renewal proposal or the relevant third-party charges. A first-year launch package is not a promise of repeated annual content production.
What is in Standard?
Cloud workspace, knowledge base, product data centre, AI employees, inquiry workflow, customer CRM, basic Customer 360, quote workflow, basic content assets, human approval and AI work training.
Is AI acquisition only in the ¥40,000 package?
No. Standard already includes self-service acquisition: lead discovery, company profiling, scoring, outreach preparation and CRM entry. Global Acquisition adds three months of configured acquisition operation and three monthly reports, alongside its website and content deliverables.
Is private deployment available?
Enterprise offers a dedicated environment and private deployment for companies with stricter data, system and compliance requirements.
Can it connect to our CRM or ERP?
Yes — through API, MCP, connectors, Activepieces, Windmill, Workspace Bridge and channel plugins; migration is part of Enterprise.
Are model costs included?
Platform capability and model / API / third-party usage are separate; each plan carries its own allowance, with overage billed on use.
How are training and implementation done?
Standard includes one setup session and one basic training session. Enterprise comes with a dedicated FDE who feeds real workflows straight back into the platform.
Which level should we start at?
Start with one workflow. Pick the work that costs the most time or growth, get it running, then decide which level you need.
What about multiple companies or brands?
Multiple departments, companies, brands and accounts belong to Enterprise: separate permissions, approvals and data boundaries on one shared AI workforce.
