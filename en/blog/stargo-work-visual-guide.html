STARGO WORK guide: from leads to operations

A guide to STARGO WORK and its core app OPEN WORK: prospecting, quotes, fulfillment, 288 AI Staff, teamwork and one-click video.

September 17, 2026 · The STARGO WORK team · View all articles

STARGO WORK is an AI workspace for manufacturers and exporters. Its core app, OPEN WORK, works like a chat: you ask, and AI looks things up and drafts across 15 apps such as the Customer CRM, Knowledge Base, Prospecting and ERP, while people approve the key actions.

Key takeaways

  • The core app, OPEN WORK, is one chat box: ask, and AI works across 15 apps, with your approval before anything goes out.
  • The journey: acquisition → trade sales → fulfillment → business coordination → repeat growth.
  • 288 AI Staff across ten functions, and they can work as a team on complex tasks.
  • Authorized people approve quotes, outreach and payments; approved is not the same as sent.
  • Start with one workflow, validate it with real cases, then expand.

This guide covers sixteen topics in business order: what each one does and what it is worth to an owner.

How does one system connect acquisition to operations?

Prospecting and the Sales Workbench lead the journey: find customers and qualify opportunities, then carry conversations, quotes, PIs and order handoffs along one sales chain. The ERP, creative work, AI Staff, the Knowledge Base and the Owner Dashboard support that line, and every outcome feeds the next growth cycle.

Value for the owner: less copying and fewer handoffs; more evidence-backed progress and results you can check.

Learn more: the full catalogue.

Prospecting: how are opportunities found proactively?

Prospecting goes beyond incoming inquiries. Around your products, markets and customer profile, it finds dealers, importers and target accounts through public search, company websites, maps, social channels, trade shows and authorized trade data; ranks them by buying signals, reorder patterns and contact research; and prepares multilingual outreach and follow-up plans. Thin evidence is flagged, never filled in.

Value for the owner: scattered prospecting becomes targeted, evidence-backed and prioritized.

Learn more: Customer acquisition.

Sales Workbench: how does a customer become an order?

The Sales Workbench takes over approved prospects and inquiries from every channel. Email, website, WhatsApp and Alibaba.com messages arrive in one place, requirements are read from messages and attachments, and records, conversations, quotes and orders form one complete customer file. Replies rest on specifications, pricing policy, MOQ and trade terms; follow-ups, reminders and human takeover prevent duplicate outreach.

Value for the owner: anyone can pick up an account with full context, from conversation to quote, order and reorder.

Learn more: Inquiries and conversations.

How are quotes, PIs and trade documents controlled?

Quotes are drafted from configuration, quantity, currency and trade terms, using price lists, quote history, discount permissions and margin guardrails; AI never invents prices. Authorized people approve prices and commitments, and an agreed quote becomes a PI with its approved version kept. Contract clause checks run on the same chain, together with the preparation and filing of commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin and Form E materials, and bills of lading.

Value for the owner: grounded prices, protected margins, versioned documents — and approval kept separate from sending.

Learn more: Quotations and PI.

ERP and commerce: can the back office keep up?

Sales and the back office share one workspace. Product records and bills of materials stay consistent, order demand links to purchasing and incoming goods, stock shows what can be delivered, production, quality and lead times connect to sales promises, and orders match invoices. Store Admin handles shipping for store orders.

Value for the owner: know whether you can deliver, when, and that the records agree afterwards.

Learn more: ERP and fulfillment.

After the sale, who follows delivery, payment and reorders?

A sale is a milestone, not the end. Deposits, production, quality checks, shipment and customer confirmation are organized by task, owner and deadline, with delays and missing records flagged. Logistics, receivables, reconciliation and export-rebate materials come with reminders, and support, warranty, spare parts and reorder opportunities are retained, with recurring issues fed back into product and sales knowledge.

Value for the owner: from acquisition to payment, support and reorders, each step feeds the next round of growth.

What can AI images and brand content do?

Starting from your own product photos and facts, logo, colors, market and channel, you generate and edit hero images, scenes, benefit posters, detail pages, catalogs and social assets. A marketing kit plans a full set of images and copy at once, inaccurate visuals or specifications can be corrected locally, and website and search content comes with multilingual and SEO and GEO planning.

Value for the owner: one set of product facts feeds sales, website, commerce and social assets.

Learn more: AI images.

How does one-click AI video work?

One brief becomes a video project. From product facts, audience, language, duration and format, a production plan is prepared for review; then the visuals are generated, voiceover, captions, music and a branded end card are added, and a portrait, landscape or square film is exported. A failed shot is redone on its own, and versions and costs are recorded.

Value for the owner: a playable, exportable video file, not just a message saying it is done.

Learn more: One-click video.

Does viral creative adaptation copy other videos?

No. It first takes an authorized reference video apart: how it hooks, how shots are paced, when benefits appear and how it asks for action. Then it rebuilds that structure around your product, brand and market as three original directions with the changes stated, followed by production, review and output in several formats. Claims come only from your verified product facts.

Value for the owner: learn what works and turn it into original content for your brand.

Learn more: Viral adaptation. No copied footage, faces, voices, music, logos or watermarks; the reference must be authorized; viral results are not guaranteed.

Which functions do the 288 AI Staff cover?

The 288 AI Staff span ten enterprise functions, not export sales alone. Each role can be configured with responsibilities, skills, enterprise knowledge, tools, access and a run history. An owner sets a goal, picks AI Staff or a team, reviews the results and approves key actions. In OPEN WORK they live in the STARGO AI Digital Office app.

Value for the owner: an AI Staff roster you choose from by task, not a stack of identical chat windows.

Learn more: the AI Staff roster. 288 is the size of the roster, not 288 people replaced; which AI Staff you use, how many work together and what they may do is set by your company, within its budget and permissions.

How do several AI Staff finish one job together?

A complex task can go to an AI Staff team. Members are chosen by role, skills and access; they send each other directed messages, ask questions, add context and hand over results; research, sales, product and content subtasks run in parallel; and a coordinating role checks and combines the work into one package for a person to confirm. Work continues after a pause or takeover.

Value for the owner: information flows, ownership is clear and the result can be handed over.

Learn more: the team scenario. Rounds, budgets and actions are capped and work can be stopped; shared task context does not grant other AI Staff members’ permissions; external actions need an authorized approval.

How does AI come to understand your company?

The Knowledge Base is the company’s reference room: products, pricing policies, certifications and contract templates carry their source and version, and answers cite them. The relationship map links customers, products, quotes, orders, tasks and owners, answering which customer this is, which price applies and who is responsible. Imported material is reviewed before it counts as company fact.

Value for the owner: AI works in your business context instead of starting from scratch.

Learn more: Memory & Evolution.

Does proactive work mean AI decides everything?

No. Proactive means watching connected customers, orders and tasks for opportunities, deadlines and exceptions, proposing an evidence-backed next step and acting within authorized scope — and pausing for a person when evidence or permission is missing. Useful experience becomes long-term memory, and improvements are tested and approved before use and withdrawn if they fall short.

Value for the owner: opportunities, deadlines and decisions come to you without being chased.

Learn more: Retained experience. Proactive never means consciousness or unrestricted autonomy.

Why is one chat box enough for the whole workspace?

OPEN WORK starts from one chat box, with the 15 apps in a sidebar beside it: Customer CRM, Knowledge Base, Sales Workbench, ERP and the rest. You say what you need, and AI goes into the right app to look things up and draft, and puts together spreadsheets, documents, translations and reports; speech becomes notes and tasks; routine web work, forms, uploads and downloads run in an authorized environment without bypassing sign-in or security checks; and email, drives, CRM and ERP connect through company authorization.

Value for the owner: less switching between tools, with entry points, files and AI Staff in one place.

Learn more: Workspace overview.

How does an owner see the business and control key actions?

The Owner Dashboard summarizes customers, opportunities, quotes, orders, approvals and exceptions; the task overview shows goals, steps, owners and results, separating work in progress, results awaiting a check and verified completion; the Control Center manages data synchronization and system status. Quotes, outreach and payments go to authorized people, and actions, costs and results are recorded within budget limits.

Value for the owner: you watch the business, not the AI.

Learn more: Security & setup. Dashboard metrics come only from connected data.

Where should a company start?

Prove one thing first, then grow: choose the workflow that matters most, prepare the context, connect authorized accounts, assign AI Staff and approvals, and validate with real cases.

Available now: everything in this guide is available in OPEN WORK, including Prospecting, automatic approval routing below the standard price, one-click AI video, viral creative adaptation, voice-to-task and authorized web work. Video generation runs within the services and credits a company enables, and people review finished videos before publishing. Using your own mailbox, channels, prices and existing systems needs your authorization to connect them, and we help set them up.

Value for the owner: real results from one workflow before deciding where to expand.

Frequently asked questions

What is STARGO WORK?

It is an AI workspace for manufacturers and exporters, and its core app is OPEN WORK. You ask in a chat, and AI works across apps such as the Customer CRM, Knowledge Base and Sales Workbench to look things up and draft; quotes and outreach wait for an authorized person.

How do Prospecting and the Sales Workbench differ?

Prospecting focuses on finding accounts, qualifying opportunities and planning engagement. The Sales Workbench focuses on understanding customers, following up, quoting and handing over orders. Approved prospects carry their context and next actions into the sales process.

Is it only for the export department?

No. Acquisition and trade sales are the starting point, while the scope also covers operations, commerce, purchasing, stock, production, quality, finance coordination, creative work, knowledge, office tasks and management. Work that uses your own systems needs your authorization to connect them.

Are one-click video and viral adaptation the same feature?

No. One-click video starts from product facts and a brief and organizes the script, storyboard, visuals, voiceover, captions and export into a playable, exportable file. Viral adaptation first studies the structure of an authorized reference video and develops three original directions for your product. Finished videos are reviewed by people before publishing.

Does viral adaptation copy someone else’s video?

No. It studies hooks, pacing, shot structure and calls to action, then develops original content for your product and brand. It does not copy footage, faces, voices, music, logos or watermarks, and it does not guarantee viral performance.

Does proactive work mean AI decides everything?

No. It means tracking customers, orders, deadlines and exceptions, recommending next steps and progressing authorized work. People remain responsible for consequential judgment.

Does it issue certificates or replace professional review?

The scope is preparation, checking, follow-up and workflow coordination — not official issuance, acceptance or approval. Payments, legal contract review and trade-compliance decisions remain with the relevant institutions or authorized people.
