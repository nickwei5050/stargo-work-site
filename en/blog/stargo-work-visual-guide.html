STARGO WORK, explained: one system from acquisition to operations

September 17, 2026 · The STARGO WORK team · View all articles

07Product guidesIllustration

STARGO WORK is a browser-based desktop AI operating system for manufacturing and global trade enterprises. Growth OS for customer acquisition and Sales Desk for trade sales form the core, backed in one workspace by operations, creative work, enterprise knowledge and 288 AI employees. People make the key decisions.

Key takeaways

  • One browser desktop, two core engines: Growth OS for customer acquisition and Sales Desk for trade sales.
  • The loop: acquisition → trade sales → fulfillment → business coordination → repeat growth.
  • 288 AI employees across ten functions; AI teamwork, one-click video and viral adaptation are available now, within the services, credits and access you enable.
  • Authorized people approve quotes, outreach and payments; approved is not the same as sent.
  • Start with one workflow, validate it with real cases, then expand from there.

This guide covers sixteen topics in business order: what each one does, what it is worth to an owner and what to know in practice.

How does one system connect acquisition to operations?

Growth OS and Sales Desk lead the business: find customers and qualify opportunities, then move conversations, quotes, PIs and order handoffs through one sales process. Operations, creative work, AI employees, enterprise knowledge and management tools all support that main workflow, and every outcome feeds the next growth cycle.

Value for the owner: less copying and fewer handoffs; more evidence-backed progress and results you can check.

In practice: every module, from acquisition to operations, is live and can be enabled as needed. See the full capability catalog.

Growth OS: how does it find opportunities proactively?

Growth OS goes beyond incoming inquiries. Based on your products, markets and customer profile, it finds dealers, importers and target accounts through public search, company websites, maps, social channels, trade shows and authorized trade data; ranks them by buying signals, reorder patterns and contact research; and prepares multilingual outreach and follow-up plans. Thin evidence is flagged, never filled in.

Value for the owner: scattered prospecting becomes targeted, evidence-backed and prioritized.

In practice: data and outreach channels connect with your authorization. See Customer acquisition.

Sales Desk: how does an inquiry become an order?

Sales Desk takes over approved prospects and inquiries from every channel. Email, website, WhatsApp and Alibaba.com messages arrive in one place, requirements are read from messages and attachments, and records, conversations, quotes and orders form a complete customer view. Replies are based on product specifications, pricing policy, MOQ and trade terms; follow-ups, reminders and human takeover prevent duplicate outreach.

Value for the owner: anyone can pick up an account with full context, from conversation to quote, order and reorder.

In practice: available now. The workspace, messaging across channels and handoffs are all live. See Inquiries and conversations.

How are quotes, PIs and trade documents controlled?

Quotes are drafted from configuration, quantity, currency and trade terms, using price lists, quote history, discount permissions and margin guardrails; AI never invents prices. Authorized people approve prices and commitments, and an agreed quote becomes a PI with its approved version kept. The same chain also covers contract clause checks and the preparation and filing of commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, Form E materials and bills of lading.

Value for the owner: grounded prices, protected margins and versioned documents, with approval kept separate from sending.

In practice: available now. Prices, contracts, signatures and documents connect to your existing systems; official documents are still issued by the relevant authorities. See Quotations and PI.

ERP and commerce: can the back office keep up?

Sales and the back office share one desktop. Product records and bills of materials stay consistent, order demand links to purchasing and incoming goods, stock shows what can be delivered, production, quality checks and lead times line up with what sales has promised, and orders match invoices. Catalogs, storefront administration and dealer portals connect as required.

Value for the owner: know whether and when you can deliver before you commit, with records that match afterwards.

In practice: available now. ERP and commerce applications are live, and so are cross-system work and AI actions. See ERP and fulfillment.

After the sale, who follows up on delivery, payment and reorders?

A sale is a milestone, not the end. Deposits, production, quality checks, shipment and customer confirmation are organized by task, owner and deadline, with delays and missing records flagged. Logistics, receivables, reconciliation and export-rebate materials come with reminders, and support, warranty, spare parts and reorder opportunities are tracked, with recurring issues fed back into product and sales knowledge.

Value for the owner: from acquisition to payment, support and reorders, each round of business feeds the next.

In practice: available now and connects to order, logistics, finance and service systems; authorized people still approve filings, payments and professional reviews.

What can AI do for images and brand content?

Starting from your own product photos and facts, logo, colors, market and channel, you generate and edit hero images, scenes, benefit posters, detail pages, catalogs and social assets. A marketing kit plans a full set of images and copy at once, inaccurate visuals or specifications can be corrected locally, and website and search content gets multilingual, SEO and GEO planning.

Value for the owner: one set of product facts feeds sales, website, commerce and social assets.

In practice: available now. The marketing kit produces a full set of images and copy in one click, with local checks and fixes; external publishing needs separate authorization. See AI images.

How does one-click AI video work?

One brief becomes a video project. From product facts, audience, language, duration and format, a production plan is prepared for review; then the visuals are generated, voiceover, captions, music and a branded end card are added, and the finished video is exported in portrait, landscape or square format. A failed shot is redone on its own, and versions and costs are recorded.

Value for the owner: a playable, exportable video file, not just a message saying it is done.

In practice: available now. People review finished videos before they are published; generation runs within the services and credits you have enabled; you must have the right to use any assets. See One-click video.

Does viral creative adaptation copy other people’s videos?

No. It first takes an authorized reference video apart: how it hooks, how shots are paced, when benefits appear and how it asks for action. Then it rebuilds that structure around your product, brand and market as three original directions with the changes stated, followed by production, review and output in several formats. Claims come only from your verified product facts.

Value for the owner: learn what works and turn it into original content for your brand.

In practice: available now. No copied footage, faces, voices, music, logos or watermarks; you must have the right to use the reference video; viral results are not guaranteed. See Viral adaptation.

Which jobs do the 288 AI employees cover?

The 288 specialized roles span ten enterprise functions, not export sales alone. Each has configurable responsibilities, skills, enterprise knowledge, tools and access, plus a run history. An owner sets a goal, picks employees or a team, reviews the results and approves key actions.

Value for the owner: a directory of professional roles chosen by task, not a stack of identical chat windows.

In practice: 288 is the size of the role directory, not a claim to replace 288 people; enabled employees, team size and permitted actions follow configuration, budget and access. See the role directory.

How do several AI employees finish one job together?

A complex task can go to an AI team. Members are chosen by role, skills and access; they send each other directed messages, ask questions, add context and hand over results; research, sales, product and content subtasks run in parallel; and a coordinating role checks and combines the work into one package for a person to confirm. Work continues after a pause or takeover.

Value for the owner: information flows, ownership is clear and the result can be handed over.

In practice: available now. Rounds, budgets and actions are capped and work can be stopped; human approval and takeover remain; shared task context does not grant other employees’ permissions; external actions need approval from an authorized person. See the team scenario.

How does AI come to understand your company?

The knowledge base is the company’s reference room: products, pricing policies, certifications and contract templates carry their source and version, and answers cite them. The relationship map links customers, products, quotes, orders, tasks and owners, answering which customer this is, which price applies and who is responsible. Imported material is reviewed before it counts as company fact.

Value for the owner: AI works with your business context, so you don’t have to explain everything each time.

In practice: available now. The knowledge base, relationship map and templates are all live. See Enterprise Intelligence.

Does proactive work mean AI decides everything?

No. Proactive means watching connected customers, orders and tasks for opportunities, deadlines and exceptions, proposing an evidence-backed next step and acting within authorized scope. When evidence or permission is missing, it stops and asks a person. Useful experience becomes long-term memory, and improvements are tested and approved before use and withdrawn if they fall short.

Value for the owner: opportunities, deadlines and decisions come to you without you having to chase them.

In practice: available now. Enterprise-wide proactive work, unified long-term memory and advanced improvement are live; proactive never means consciousness or unrestricted autonomy. See Retained experience.

Why is a browser enough for the whole workspace?

It works like a dedicated company computer: application windows, files and AI employees share one desktop. AI helps with spreadsheets, documents, translation and reports; speech becomes notes and tasks; routine web work, forms, uploads and downloads run in an authorized environment without bypassing sign-in or security checks; and email, drives, CRM and ERP connect through company authorization.

Value for the owner: less switching between tools, with entry points, files and AI employees in one place.

In practice: available now. Open a browser and start working; voice, automation, external actions and other clients are live too. See Workspace overview.

How does an owner see the business and control key actions?

The owner’s cockpit summarizes customers, opportunities, quotes, orders, approvals and exceptions; the task overview shows goals, steps, owners and results, separating work in progress, results awaiting a check and verified completion; the control center manages data synchronization and system status. Quotes, outreach and payments go to authorized people for approval; actions, costs and results are all recorded, and budget limits can be set.

Value for the owner: you watch the business, not the AI.

In practice: available now. Dashboards and automated actions run on connected data, and metrics come only from that data. See Enterprise control and delivery.

Where should you start, and how do you expand?

Prove one thing first, then grow: choose the workflow that matters most, prepare the context, connect authorized accounts, assign employees and approvals, and validate with real cases.

Value for the owner: real results from one workflow before deciding where to expand.

In practice: delivery covers the scope you agree on and can extend to other industries. See Pricing.

Frequently asked questions

What is STARGO WORK?

It is a browser-based desktop AI operating system for manufacturing and global trade enterprises. Growth OS and Sales Desk lead the business journey, supported by operations, creative work, enterprise knowledge and AI roles, with people retaining approval and decision authority.

How do Growth OS and Sales Desk differ?

Growth OS focuses on finding accounts, qualifying opportunities and planning engagement. Sales Desk focuses on understanding customers, following up, quoting and handing over orders. Approved prospects carry their context and next actions into the sales process.

Is it only for the export department?

No. Acquisition and trade sales are the starting point, while the scope also covers operations, commerce, purchasing, stock, production, quality, finance coordination, creative work, knowledge, office tasks and management. Once your existing systems are connected, all of this work can be done directly in STARGO WORK.

Are one-click video and viral adaptation the same feature?

No. One-click video starts from product facts and a brief and organizes the script, storyboard, visuals, voiceover, captions and export into a playable, exportable file. Viral adaptation first studies the structure of an authorized reference video and develops three original directions for your product. Both are available now, and finished videos are reviewed by people before publishing.

Does viral adaptation copy someone else’s video?

No. It studies hooks, pacing, shot structure and calls to action, then develops original content for your product and brand. It does not copy footage, faces, voices, music, logos or watermarks, and it does not guarantee viral performance.

Does proactive work mean AI decides everything?

No. It means tracking customers, orders, deadlines and exceptions, recommending next steps and progressing authorized work. Enterprise-wide proactive work, unified long-term memory and advanced improvement follow the same permission rules, and people still make the key decisions.

Is every channel and feature available right away?

Yes. AI teamwork, one-click video and viral adaptation are live, and applications such as the browser desktop, customer management, enterprise knowledge, Sales Desk, ERP and commerce are ready to use. Everything runs within the services, credits and access you enable, and channels, customer handoffs, prices, logistics and finance work as soon as they are connected to your own accounts and systems.

Can it automatically issue certificates, file customs declarations, claim tax rebates or replace professional review?

No. It handles preparation, checking, follow-up and workflow coordination, not official issuance, acceptance or approval. Payments, legal contract review and trade-compliance decisions remain with the relevant institutions or authorized people.
