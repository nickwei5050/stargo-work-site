What is an AI workspace for exporters?

STARGO WORK is an AI workspace for export manufacturers; OPEN WORK is its core app. How it differs from chat tools, what its 15 apps do and how work flows.

July 16, 2026 · The STARGO WORK team · View all articles

An AI workspace for exporters connects one chat box to all of a company’s business apps. You ask for something in the chat, AI looks things up, drafts and organizes files in apps such as the Customer CRM, Knowledge Base, Sales Workbench and ERP, and anything that goes outside the company waits for an authorized person. STARGO WORK is built for manufacturers and exporters, and its core app is called OPEN WORK.

Key takeaways

  • Ask in a chat, and AI works across 15 apps such as the Customer CRM, Knowledge Base and ERP.
  • The main line is Prospecting and the Sales Workbench: find customers, then carry quotes and PIs to orders.
  • The journey: acquisition → trade sales → fulfillment → business coordination → repeat growth.
  • Anything going to a customer, and every key decision, is approved by an authorized person.

Why is it not just one more tool?

Manufacturers and exporters do not lack software. Email receives the inquiry, a chat app holds the conversation, a spreadsheet keeps the quote and the ERP tracks the order. What is missing is the layer that connects the work — one that knows how customers, inquiries, quotes, orders and tasks relate, and moves work forward on top of those relationships.

The goal is fewer disconnected handoffs, not another tool: less copying and re-explaining, more evidence-backed progress, clear ownership and results you can check.

How is it different from a standalone AI chat?

  • It works with company context. Product facts, customer history, pricing policy and business rules are shared ground, so nobody has to explain them from scratch each time.
  • It is tied to business records and owners. Work lands on customer records, quotes, orders and tasks, each with a clear owner and next step.
  • Results are checked. Authorized people approve consequential actions, and a text answer is never treated as finished work.

What do the two main lines in OPEN WORK do?

Prospecting answers where to find customers, who is worth pursuing and how to reach them next. It looks for dealers, importers, wholesalers and target accounts around your products, markets and buyer types, and turns research, contacts, buying signals and priorities into a plan. Approved prospects move into the Sales Workbench with their context and next actions.

The Sales Workbench answers how to understand a customer, keep following up, quote and hand over the order. It brings inquiries and messages into one intake and puts customer records, conversations, product fit, reply drafts, follow-ups, quotes and PIs on one sales chain.

How do the other apps support those two lines?

  • ERP and fulfillment: products, materials, purchasing, stock, production, quality, orders, invoices and shipping for store orders, followed by logistics, finance, documents and support.
  • Content growth: product images, detail pages, catalogs and multilingual content, plus one-click video and viral creative adaptation.
  • 288 AI Staff: specialized roles across ten enterprise functions that can team up by task, dispatched from the STARGO AI Digital Office.
  • Knowledge Base and memory: what the company knows, how things connect and who is responsible.
  • Owner Dashboard and control: priorities, exceptions and approvals, with costs and results you can check.

What does the complete business journey look like?

Proactive acquisition finds and qualifies customers. Trade sales handles conversations, quotes and PIs. Fulfillment connects purchasing, production and stock. Business coordination follows payments, support and channels. Repeat growth watches reorders, reactivation and reviews. Customer context and outcomes then feed the next cycle.

Can we use it today?

Yes. All 15 apps in OPEN WORK are ready to use, with Prospecting, the Sales Workbench, AI Staff teamwork, one-click video, viral creative adaptation and voice-to-task among them; external actions need an authorized approval. What each app does is in the product catalog. Using your own mailbox, channels and existing systems needs your authorization to connect them, and we help set them up.

For reminders and memory, see Proactive Reminders & Memory; for every feature, see Product; for the roster, see AI Staff.

Frequently asked questions

What is STARGO WORK?

It is an AI workspace for manufacturers and exporters, and its core app is OPEN WORK. You ask in a chat, and AI works across apps such as the Customer CRM, Knowledge Base and Sales Workbench to look things up and draft; quotes and outreach wait for an authorized person.

How is this different from using a standalone AI chat?

The focus is not the chat format. It is whether the work connects enterprise information, customer history, business applications, ownership, approvals and results. STARGO WORK organizes those elements around a business workflow rather than equating a text answer with completed work.

Is it only for the export department?

No. Acquisition and trade sales are the starting point, while the scope also covers operations, commerce, purchasing, stock, production, quality, finance coordination, creative work, knowledge, office tasks and management. Work that uses your own systems needs your authorization to connect them.

Do we need to install a client?

No. OPEN WORK runs in the browser.
