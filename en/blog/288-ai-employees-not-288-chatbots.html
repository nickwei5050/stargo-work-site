288 AI roles: how AI employees team up, talk and deliver

July 2, 2026 · The STARGO WORK team · View all articles

288 is the size of STARGO WORK’s directory of specialized AI roles across ten enterprise functions. For complex work, several AI employees can form a team, message each other and work in parallel, and a coordinating role consolidates their work into a single result for a person to review. This teamwork is available now.

Key takeaways

  • 288 is the number of specialized roles across ten functions, not 288 people replaced.
  • AI employees can team up now: direct messages, parallel work, one consolidated result.
  • Rounds, budgets and actions are capped, work can be stopped at any time, and people can always approve or take over.
  • Shared task context is not shared permission; external actions need approval from an authorized person.

Which functions do the 288 roles cover?

288 counts the roles in the directory; it is not a claim to replace 288 people. Which employees are enabled, how large a collaboration can be and which actions are permitted all depend on your configuration, budget and access.

How is an AI employee different from a chat window?

A chat window starts from a blank conversation and ends after the answer. An AI employee can be configured with responsibilities, skills, enterprise knowledge, permitted tools, access, tasks and a run history. It knows what it is responsible for, which company information it may use and which actions must be cleared with a person first.

How is an AI team formed?

Once a manager sets a business goal, suitable employees are matched by role, skills, enterprise knowledge, tool access and task complexity, and a small team is formed with explicit responsibilities: who researches, who plans the sales approach, who checks the product, who prepares the assets and who reviews and coordinates.

How do team members talk to each other?

  • Directed messages: one employee can message or question another directly — asking the product specialist to check specifications, for example.
  • Context and handoffs: members add information, pass on questions and hand over results, so people no longer copy every message between them.
  • Progress updates: each member’s progress is visible, and a blocked step is easy to spot.
  • Parallel work: research, sales, product, content and data tasks run at the same time; the coordinating role checks them and combines them into one deliverable.

Each task keeps its goal, owner, claim status, deadline, evidence, failure reasons and next step, so work continues after a pause, a human takeover or a recovery.

What are the limits on teamwork?

  • Collaboration rounds, budgets and permitted actions are capped, which prevents looping discussions, duplicate work and unauthorized operations.
  • Work can be stopped at any time, and approval, pausing, takeover and result checks always stay with people.
  • Shared task context does not open all company data, and it never gives one employee another employee’s permissions.
  • External actions, such as messaging a customer, need approval from an authorized person.

How does an owner delegate?

Five steps: set the business goal → select employees or a team → assign work → inspect the outputs → approve key actions. When judgment is needed or something unusual happens, a person takes over.

Frequently asked questions

What does “288 digital employees” mean?

It denotes 288 specialized, task-selectable roles across ten enterprise role groups — not trade sales alone. Each role can be configured with responsibilities, knowledge, skills and access, and several can form a team, message each other and complete one task together. The number is not a claim to replace 288 people; the employees enabled, the size of a collaboration and permitted actions depend on configuration, budget and access.

How do AI employees work together?

They divide a shared goal into roles, message each other, ask questions and hand over context, and work on research, products, sales or content in parallel. A coordinating role checks and consolidates the results. Ownership, deadlines, next steps and human takeover stay visible; rounds and budgets are capped, and the work can be stopped at any time.

Does a company enable all 288 roles at once?

Not necessarily. Which employees are enabled, how large a collaboration is and which actions are allowed follow the company’s configuration, budget and access. You can start with the few roles one workflow needs.

Can an AI team message customers on its own?

No. External actions need approval from an authorized person. The team can prepare prospect lists, outreach plans and message drafts, and your company rules decide whether anything is sent.
