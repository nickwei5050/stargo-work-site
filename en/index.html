(FAQ)

It is a browser-based desktop AI operating system for manufacturing and global trade enterprises. Growth OS (prospecting) and Sales Desk (trade sales) form the core, connected to operations, creative work, enterprise knowledge and AI employees, while people keep approval and the final say.

Not necessarily. STARGO WORK connects your existing accounts and information in one workspace. Which systems you keep, connect or adjust depends on your current software and access permissions.

It means 288 specialized roles, picked by task, across ten enterprise functions, not just trade sales. Each role can be configured with responsibilities, knowledge, skills and access, and several can form a team, message each other and complete one task together. The number is not a claim to replace 288 people; the employees enabled, the size of a collaboration and permitted actions depend on configuration, budget and access.

People with approval rights do. AI can organize information, prepare drafts and recommend next steps. Quotes, outreach, important commitments, official submissions and payments follow your approval rules. Approving, sending and checking the outcome are separate steps.
