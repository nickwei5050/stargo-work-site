Security & setup · for owners and IT

Delegate the work. Keep the authority.

Which actions need a person’s approval, who can see what, how every step is recorded and how it connects to the systems you already use. The owner sees the results in the Owner Dashboard and deals with the real decisions first.

Approvals

Key actions, approved by people.

Your company decides which quotes, customer outreach and published content need approval, and who approves them. Payments, official filings and professional reviews always stay with authorized people.

These need a person’s yes

  • Replies and outreach
  • Quotes and PIs
  • Below standard price
  • Important commitments
  • Payments
  • Official filings
  • Contract and compliance review

  1. Nothing leaves without approval

    Every reply, outreach email and PI starts as a draft. It goes out only after someone with authority clicks Approve.

  2. Prices come from your price list

    Quotes use your price list and knowledge base. Anything below your standard price goes to the sales manager and is not sent before approval. Your company sets the approver and the price floor.

Automation guardrails

Automation, with limits.

Scheduled work, event triggers and data sync are run by hand first and their rules agreed before more of it is automated. A step that goes outside the company still stops for your approval.

  • Stops and reports on failure

    On an exception, work stops for a person to take over or send back.

  • Work limits for AI Staff

    What they may use, and on whose behalf, is set in advance.

  • Small trials first

    Tried on part of the work first, and withdrawn if it falls short.

Management controls

Who can see and do what, set in advance.

Five management components govern how AI works; the 13 controls below are what they cover in detail.

  • Capability & app management

    What is enabled, what work is possible, what still needs setup, and the usage and budget limits.

  • Account & role permissions

    Who may read information, and who may change records.

  • Approval of key actions

    Quotes, outreach and important commitments go to authorized reviewers.

  • Work & result records

    What was done, who approved it and whether the result met the requirement; failures stop and pass to a person.

  • Linked accounts & protection

    Business accounts connect through company authorization, with no access for roles that do not need it.

13 controls

  • application access
  • account and role permissions
  • approval of key actions
  • activity and approval records
  • secure keeping of sign-in credentials
  • work limits for AI Staff
  • data kept apart between companies
  • stop and report on failure
  • small trials before wider use
  • reversal when results fall short
  • result checks
  • visible progress
  • human takeover

Connect what you already use, one authorization at a time.
