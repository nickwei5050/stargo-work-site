# Rototo Studio (Webflow template export)

Supplied by the site owner on 2026-09-09 as `rototo-website-template.zip`, to
provide the rotating image display on the homepage. Vendored the way every other
donor is: the export's own `index.html`, `css/` and `js/`, unmodified.

What this site takes from it: the homepage hero's rotating gallery —
`.home-hero-wrapper` holding `.home-header-group.first/.second/.third`, each of
which carries `.home-header-img-wrap` tiles, driven by the template's ix3 (GSAP)
timelines rather than by IX2.

The owner asked for the template's own photographs to be kept ("用 rototo 原图"),
so the images are mirrored as they are rather than swapped for this site's
artwork. Only text changes.
