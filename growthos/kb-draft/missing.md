# 官网上没有的内容 / What the site does not say

AI 客服不能回答下面这些，应转人工。老板补充并批准后，才能写进知识库。

The AI must hand these to a person. They can be added only after the owner supplies and approves them.

| 缺什么 | Missing |
|---|---|
| 各方案价格、折扣（官网写“联系报价”） | Plan prices and discounts (the site says "Ask us") |

| 从签约到开通需要多久、各阶段周期 | Time from contract to go-live, and per-stage timelines |

| 产品界面和 AI 回复支持哪些语言 | Languages of the product interface and AI replies |
