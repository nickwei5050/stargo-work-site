## 1. What STARGO WORK is

STARGO WORK brings disconnected business work into one browser-based desktop. Lead Finder (proactive customer acquisition) and Sales Desk (trade sales) are the two core engines; ERP and fulfillment, AI creative work, 288 specialized AI roles, enterprise knowledge and the business relationship map, proactive work and memory, the browser desktop and the owner’s cockpit with its controls support them. Principle: delegate the work, keep the authority. Each company’s capabilities are enabled to match its configuration and agreed delivery scope.

## 3. Lead Finder, Sales Desk, quotes, ERP

- Lead Finder finds dealers, importers, wholesalers and target accounts, researches them, ranks opportunities and prepares outreach plans; approved prospects move into Sales Desk with their context. Data and outreach channels connect only with authorization.
- Sales Desk brings inquiries and messages into one intake and carries customer records, product fit, replies, follow-up, quotes and PI to the order handoff. The workspace is live, and channel messaging and business handoffs work through the company’s own accounts.
- Quotes and PI follow company-approved prices, discount permissions and margin guardrails. Live prices, contracts, signatures and documents come from the company’s own systems; approval and sending are separate steps.
- The ERP is live, and so are cross-system work and AI actions. Fulfillment, finance, logistics and service run through connected systems; filings, payments and professional reviews are confirmed by authorized people.

Source: https://stargowork.com/en/capabilities

## 4. Creative work, knowledge and proactive work, the browser desktop

- One-click AI video is available: brief, script, storyboard, visuals, voiceover, captions and export of a playable, exportable file in portrait, landscape or square, with a production plan reviewed before generation, failed shots redone individually and every version and cost recorded. Finished videos are reviewed by people before publishing; generation runs within the services and credits the company enables; assets must be ones the company may use.
- Viral creative adaptation (not yet open to customers, pending a commercial licence): an authorized reference video is analyzed for structure and rebuilt around your product and brand as three original directions. It never copies footage, faces, voices, music, logos or watermarks, and viral performance is not guaranteed.
- AI images and brand content: the creative workspace is live, with one-click marketing-kit production and local checks; publishing to external channels needs separate authorization.
- Enterprise knowledge and the business relationship map are live, and the enterprise templates are ready; the more sources you connect, the richer the relationships. Proactive work, unified long-term memory and advanced improvement are live too; proactive work is not consciousness or unrestricted autonomy.
- The browser desktop is the main way in; voice, automation and external actions are supported, and inquiries and approvals also work in a phone browser. Owner dashboards show metrics only from connected data.

## 5. AI workforce

- The role directory lists 288 specialized AI roles in ten enterprise functions: Enterprise essentials 15, Customer development & marketing 50, Sales 16, Customer service 5, Risk & compliance 20, Supply chain 4, Finance 14, Operations 19, Product & engineering 129, Professional services 16 (total 288). It is not a claim to replace 288 people; the employees enabled, the size of a collaboration and permitted actions follow the company’s configuration, budget and access.
- AI teamwork is available: several AI employees form a team for one task, message each other, work in parallel, and a coordinating role checks and consolidates one result for a person to confirm. Collaboration rounds, budgets and permitted actions are capped, and work can be stopped at any time. Human approval, pause and takeover always remain; sharing task context does not share permissions, and external actions need an approver’s sign-off.

## 6. What each plan includes (no prices)

There are no prices on the site because team size, apps, server and service scope differ from company to company, so a formal quote is sent for what is actually needed.

- Core: for small export teams that want inquiries, AI replies and quotes working first. A dedicated 16GB server per company, kept apart from other companies; one owner and 4 staff accounts, each salesperson sees only their own customers; AI workbench, knowledge base, office documents, inquiry intake, Sales Desk and ERP; inquiry mailbox and platform inquiry emails connected; 3,000 AI credits a month.
- Professional: for larger factories and trading companies that want the full app suite. Everything in Core, plus a dedicated 32GB server; one owner and 14 staff accounts; the full suite (Lead Finder, workflows, canvas, video studio and more); more inquiry channels, 10,000 AI credits a month and priority support.
- Flagship: for companies with more data and people, and integrations of their own. Everything in Core and Professional, plus a larger server as needed; accounts and monthly AI credits set by contract; custom integrations and historical data migration; on-site training.
- Marketing system: our team builds a complete inbound marketing system, scoped by contract, with no ranking guarantees. A multilingual B2B website build; SEO, GEO and AEO content and structure work; articles published through the year; overseas social media content; an AI support assistant on the website.
- Private custom: for larger companies that deploy on their own servers and need deep customization. Existing CRM and ERP integration and migration; workflows and AI employees built around your process; permissions and approvals across departments, companies and brands; signed as a project and delivered by milestone.

The 288 AI employees are not unlimited. The workforce count describes the capability catalog; actual access, active workloads, concurrency, credits and third-party usage are set by the contracted configuration. Professional and Flagship install the full app suite, including Lead Finder.

Which level to start at: start with one workflow. Pick the work that costs the most time or growth, get it running, then decide which plan you need.

## 8. Controls, approvals and checking results

- Human control: authorized people approve quotes, outreach, important commitments, official filings and payments. Approved is not sent, and sent is not received or done.

Key decisions stay with the company. You decide which quotes, customer outreach and published content need approval, and who approves them. Payments, official filings and professional reviews always stay with authorized people. Who did what, who approved it, whether the result met the requirement and where a failure stopped are all on record. Submitted is not finished until the result is checked.

13 management controls: application access, account and role permissions, approval of key actions, activity and approval records, secure central storage of sign-in credentials, work limits for AI roles, data kept apart between companies, stop and report on failure, small trials before wider use, reversal when results fall short, result checks, visible progress and human takeover.

The owner's cockpit brings opportunity progress, delivery status, usage and budget limits, pending approvals, task owners, blockers and actual results together. Figures come only from connected systems; anything missing is shown as missing.
